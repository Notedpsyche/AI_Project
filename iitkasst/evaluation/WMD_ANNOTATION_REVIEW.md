# WMD evaluation annotation review — proposals only

**Status:** not approved ground truth. This review does not change `evaluation/judgments.jsonl`, the retrieval implementation, or benchmark scores. Proposed machine-readable labels are in [wmd_proposed_annotations.jsonl](wmd_proposed_annotations.jsonl); they deliberately use `proposal_status`, not `review_status: "reviewed"`.

## Method

- Candidate traces were produced on 23 October 2026 from the current local BM25 + BGE + RRF indexes.
- The tables show the first three metadata-eligible RRF candidates, except where fewer exist. Text is a verbatim review excerpt from the indicated chunk; source links open the official page/PDF.
- Proposed grade scale: **3** = directly answers the question or supplies an indispensable part of a multi-source answer; **2** = useful supporting/duplicate evidence; **1** = topical but insufficient; **0** = not relevant. No grade is proposed where the correct outcome is abstention or the unit of relevance needs redesign.
- A positive retrieval label is not itself approval to generate an answer. The expected behaviour and any structural ambiguity are recorded per case.
